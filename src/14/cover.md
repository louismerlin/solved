# PSYCHORAPTURE, pt. 2 Cover Challenge

This challenge can be found on the cover of issue 14. Spot the long line at the bottom.

## The Line

This criss-crossed line means nothing to me, but again [dcode's symbols cipher list](https://www.dcode.fr/symbols-ciphers) comes in for the win!

After a bit of scrolling I recognized the [Ogham Alphabet](https://www.dcode.fr/ogham-alphabet) (notice the main line is vertical whereas it is horizontal on the cover), which is an ancient Irish/Pictish alphabet.

Deciphering the line, we get the URL which shows us the flag.
