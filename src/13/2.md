# PSYCHORAPTURE, pt. 1 Challenge #2 - Council

This challenge can be found when Wheeler describes the Council in the car.

## The Setup

> What kind of records might the World Standards Council store?
>
> Help us find the name of the original registrant of the bestpentestcompany.com domain who bought it in 2017.
>
> The flag will NOT be in the standard format. Simply enter the first and last names, for example "Jane Doe".

No file for this one, just a domain name: `bestpentestcompany.com`. This is an OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) challenge.

## The Records

The obvious first move is to look up who owns the domain today.
Going to [whois.com](https://www.whois.com) and searching for the domain shows us the contact info for Porkbun, a domain registrar.

This is due to the [GDPR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Data_Protection_Regulation), which came into force in May 2018.
Registrars have redacted registrant details in WHOIS since then.

A domain bought in **2017** was registered *before* that, so the original record will most likely contains a real name; we just need a service that archives old WHOIS records, such as [whoxy](https://www.whoxy.com/).

Its history for the domain shows three records:

Nice, we now have the name of the person who brought the domain name back in 2017! This is our flag.
